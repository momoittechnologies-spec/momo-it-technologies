## Module 2: Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) Performance Architecture

### 2.1 The Modern Meta Algorithm (Broad Targeting & Creative-Led Ads)
In 2026, over-narrowing interests hurts ad delivery. Meta's Andromeda and Advantage+ algorithms are creative-driven. **The creative IS the targeting.**

* **The 5-Part High-Converting Ad Creative Framework:**
  1. **Hook (0 – 3 seconds):** Stop the scroll with a shocking stat, contrarian opinion, or relatable problem (*"Stop wasting ₹30,000/month on generic digital marketing that brings zero leads..."*).
  2. **Problem Agitation:** Highlight the pain point and missed revenue.
  3. **The Solution:** Introduce the client's service or product as the definitive answer.
  4. **Proof / Credibility:** Customer testimonials, metrics, before/after results.
  5. **Direct Call to Action (CTA):** Send directly to a WhatsApp chat or fast-loading mobile landing page.

### 2.2 Meta Conversions API (CAPI)
With browser third-party cookie restrictions, client-side pixel tracking loses up to 30% of conversion events. Server-side tracking via Meta Conversions API (CAPI) sends purchase and lead events directly from the server to Meta's servers, restoring 98%+ attribution accuracy.

## Module 5: Generative AI for Growth Automation

### 5.1 AI Copywriting Prompting Framework (RTCO)
Never use generic prompts like `"write an ad for a school"`. Use the RTCO Framework:
* **R (Role):** *"Act as a world-class direct-response copywriter specialized in Indian private education."*
* **T (Task):** *"Write 3 Meta ad variations promoting admissions for Sri Chaitanya School in Kadapa."*
* **C (Context):** *"Target parents aged 28–45 who prioritize IIT/NEET foundation and spoken English."*
* **O (Output):** *"Format with Hook, 3 Bullet Points with emoji benefits, and a WhatsApp CTA."*

## Module 7: Top 10 MNC Digital Marketing & Growth Interview Questions

1. **Q: What is ROAS and how do you calculate it?**  
   *A:* Return on Ad Spend. $ROAS = \frac{Revenue\,Generated\,from\,Ads}{Ad\,Spend}$. A 5x ROAS means ₹50,000 generated from ₹10,000 spent on advertising.
2. **Q: What is the difference between CPM, CPC, and CPA?**  
   *A:* CPM (Cost per Mille): cost per 1,000 impressions. CPC (Cost per Click): cost when a user clicks an ad. CPA (Cost per Acquisition): cost incurred to acquire a paying customer or qualified lead.
3. **Q: How do you optimize for Google's Helpful Content System (HCS)?**  
   *A:* Focus on first-hand experience, original data, client case studies, clear author expertise, and avoid automated thin AI spam.
4. **Q: What is A/B testing in ad campaigns and what makes it statistically significant?**  
   *A:* Testing two variations (e.g., Ad Creative A vs B) with a single variable changed. A minimum sample size of conversions is required to achieve 95%+ statistical confidence before picking a winner.
5. **Q: How do you handle ad fatigue on Meta Ads?**  
   *A:* When frequency rises above 2.5–3.0 and CPA increases, rotate creatives by introducing new hooks, angles, formats (UGC vs carousel), or refreshing the lead offer.
