Module 4: Top 5 MNC Digital Marketing Interview Questions

Q1: What is ROAS and how do you calculate it?
Return on Ad Spend = (Revenue generated from advertising) / (Total ad spend). A 5x ROAS means ₹5 earned for every ₹1 spent on ads.
Q2: What is the difference between CPM, CPC, and CPA?
CPM is cost per 1,000 impressions. CPC is cost per single click. CPA is cost per acquisition (paying customer or verified lead).
Q3: How do you prevent ad fatigue in Meta campaigns?
When frequency exceeds 2.5–3.0 and CPA starts rising, introduce new creative hooks, test user-generated video (UGC), or switch up the core offer.
